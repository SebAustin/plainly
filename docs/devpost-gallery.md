# Devpost gallery: images and captions

The image gallery on the Devpost submission, in order. Images are 2400×1600
(3:2); Devpost allows captions of up to 140 characters. The image files are not
in the repo.

| # | Image | Caption |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 3 | `gallery-03-glossary.jpg` | What you need to do as a checklist with the deadline, plus the letter's jargon explained in plain words. |
